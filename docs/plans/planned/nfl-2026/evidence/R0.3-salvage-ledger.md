## Disposition vocabulary

| Disposition | Meaning in this ledger |
|---|---|
| upstream | Content/commit is already reachable from current `origin/main`; no port is needed. |
| port unchanged | Safe to carry over verbatim after the R0 branch is ready and contracts permit it. |
| reimplement against new contracts | Retain the behavior/lesson, but rebuild under the shared fantasy/provider contracts; do not cherry-pick the legacy surface. |
| defer | Valuable but intentionally scheduled for a later roadmap task or blocked by a missing contract. |
| reject | Do not carry forward: conflicts with locked decisions, duplicates a shared capability, or is operationally unsafe. |
