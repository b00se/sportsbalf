## Contract

`src/nfl/data/identity.py` provides a deterministic, offline identity graph
for players, teams, and games. Every mapping is keyed by explicit Underdog ID
and positive season. Resolution returns typed `resolved`, `unresolved`, or
`ambiguous` outcomes. Names are retained as diagnostics only and cannot
resolve a record. Missing identifiers, invalid seasons, season mismatches,
and one UD ID mapping to multiple GSIS IDs fail closed. The material-player
report is suitable for an export gate and is ready only when every requested
player resolves exactly once.

`tests/testdata/nfl_identity_graph_fixture.json` with SHA-256
`db699cc9f5811e6dbd79c40e67abbb521ccbfa4cfe90afa07e21650596cdc3ed`.

## Repair attempt one

The graph now ingests UD player/appearance, team, and game IDs together with
GSIS and optional nflverse/consensus IDs. Records require a positive season,
GSIS ID, and at least one source ID; malformed dates fail with a typed
`IdentityIngestionError`. Lookups support week, game, and effective-date
scopes for traded-player/team-at-date cases. Name matching is opt-in via
`allow_name_review`, reports its method and similarity confidence, and returns
an ambiguous human-review outcome even above threshold; below-threshold
matches remain unresolved. `check_material_players(..., unattended=True)` is
the production/export gate and raises when any material player is unresolved
or ambiguous.
