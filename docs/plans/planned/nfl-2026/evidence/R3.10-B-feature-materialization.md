# R3.10-B feature materialization

Status: Implemented; R3.9 and R4 remain blocked

## Scope

This packet fixes the confirmed lookahead in
`src/nfl/features/qb_features.py`. Team `pass_rate_over_expected` now compares
the prior team-game rate with a league neutral pass rate computed from rows in
completed weeks strictly before the target week. A later PBP perturbation
therefore cannot change any earlier materialized row. Team and opponent outputs
copy the input dataframe `attrs`, including an archive reference supplied by
the verified archive adapter.

The materializer validates non-null numeric season/week values, an NFL week
range, non-empty canonical game/team keys, and duplicate
`season/week/game_id/play_id` keys when `play_id` is present (independent of
offense/defense team). Invalid inputs fail closed with `KeyError` or
`ValueError`.

## Evidence

Offline regression coverage is in
`tests/test_nfl_qb_features_asof.py` and uses deterministic in-memory PBP:

- future-week row perturbation leaves every earlier team and opponent feature
  unchanged;
- shuffled source rows produce identical sorted outputs;
- week-two and week-three targets match exact completed-week prior-rate
  calculations and retain the `archive_reference` attribute; and
- missing keys, invalid weeks, and duplicate canonical play keys fail closed.

Commands and results:

```text
.venv\Scripts\pytest.exe -q tests/test_nfl_qb_features_asof.py

.venv\Scripts\ruff.exe check src/nfl/features/qb_features.py tests/test_nfl_qb_features_asof.py
```

This slice proves feature-level as-of behavior only. It does not establish
multi-season archive coverage, Underdog or consensus baselines, rolling-origin
metrics, or the R3.9 promotion gate. Consequently it does not unblock R3.9 or
R4.
