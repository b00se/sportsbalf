# R3.10 NFLverse ingestion and cleaning audit

Status: Blocked pending archive packet; R4 not allowed

## Scope

Read-only audit of the current provider, cleaning, feature, and pipeline paths.
No production code, configuration, tests, or protected data directories were
changed. The requested historical source is feasible through nflverse, but an
archive backtest still needs a reproducible snapshot boundary and separately
available market baselines.

## Findings

### What can be reused

- `NFLReadPyProvider` loads `load_player_stats(..., summary_level="week")`,
  schedules, PBP, and passing NGS and normalizes their known aliases.
- `reconcile_seasons` fails closed on missing season provenance, filters
  foreign seasons, and emits typed missing-season metadata.
- `qb_attempts.py` combines those surfaces and calls feature builders for
  player history, PBP efficiency, team/opponent rates, NGS, and schedule
  context. Several per-team/player fields are shifted, but this is not a
  blanket as-of guarantee: `compute_team_and_opponent_features` computes the
  season-wide league neutral pass rate before shifting team rows. That makes
  `pass_rate_over_expected` depend on future PBP and is a confirmed leakage
  defect for R3.9.
- Existing provider tests and `tests/testdata/nflreadpy_provider_manifest.json`
  provide offline contract fixtures, not an archive of real weekly slates.

### Exact gaps before the full R3 backtest

1. The builder's parquet cache is keyed only by a year range and has no
   content manifest, source retrieval timestamp, schema fingerprint, or
   verified as-of cutoff. A cache hit can therefore not prove provenance or
   immutability for R3.9.
2. Weekly, schedule, PBP, and NGS loads are individually available, but there
   is no single archive command that atomically records all source bytes,
   versions, joins, row counts, and hashes for one evaluation corpus.
3. The current QB builder is narrower than the R3 component scope: it is not a
   complete RB/WR/TE outcomes/features archive and does not itself materialize
   identity/availability/scoring/ranking fold inputs.
4. `compute_team_and_opponent_features` calculates
   `league_neutral_pass_rate` over the full season before shifting the team
   rows. A later week's pass/rush mix therefore changes an earlier week's
   `pass_rate_over_expected`; this confirmed future-row leakage must be fixed
   and covered by a future-row perturbation regression before any backtest.
5. Calendar week ordering is not a complete timestamp contract. R3.9 needs a
   declared slate cutoff and source timestamps, then must reject rows created
   or revised after that cutoff. Provider retrieval time alone is insufficient
   to recreate historical information availability.
6. The in-repository fixtures are intentionally tiny. They cannot establish
   three outer weeks, multi-season coverage, complete player slates, or
   confidence intervals.
7. NFLverse supplies outcomes and football statistics only. No historical
   Underdog projection/line archive or independent-consensus projection source
   is present or implied by these loaders. Those baselines remain unavailable
   until separately acquired with identity mapping, timestamps, and terms
   evidence.

The following offline perturbation probe was also run against the current
feature helper:

```text
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -c "import pandas as pd; from src.nfl.features.qb_features import compute_team_and_opponent_features; rows=[{'season':2023,'week':1,'game_id':'g1','posteam':'A','defteam':'B','pass_attempt':1,'rush_attempt':0,'score_differential':0,'qtr':1},{'season':2023,'week':2,'game_id':'g2','posteam':'A','defteam':'B','pass_attempt':1,'rush_attempt':0,'score_differential':0,'qtr':1},{'season':2023,'week':3,'game_id':'g3','posteam':'A','defteam':'B','pass_attempt':0,'rush_attempt':1,'score_differential':0,'qtr':1}]; a,_=compute_team_and_opponent_features(pd.DataFrame(rows)); rows[2]['pass_attempt']=1; rows[2]['rush_attempt']=0; b,_=compute_team_and_opponent_features(pd.DataFrame(rows)); print(float(a.loc[a.week.eq(2),'pass_rate_over_expected'].iloc[0]), float(b.loc[b.week.eq(2),'pass_rate_over_expected'].iloc[0]))"
```

Result: `0.33333333333333337 0.0`. Changing only Week 3 changes the already
shifted Week 2 feature, reproducing the future-row leakage. The R3.10-B
cleaning packet must repair this and add the equivalent automated regression;
existing shift operations must not be treated as sufficient evidence.

## Gate decision

**R4 blocked.** The internal source path is promising and reusable, but the
current checkout lacks a verified multi-season, multi-surface immutable
archive and lacks historical Underdog and independent-consensus baselines.
R4 may be reconsidered only after R3.9 reports at least three eligible outer
weeks, all required metrics and uncertainty, leakage/as-of checks, source
manifests, and an explicit promotion decision.
