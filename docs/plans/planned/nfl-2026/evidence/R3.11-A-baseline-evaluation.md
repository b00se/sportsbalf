# R3.11-A baseline evaluation

Status: Implemented (offline evaluator and focused tests)

The baseline evaluator in `src/nfl/models/baseline_evaluation.py` provides two
strictly as-of protocols over a supplied canonical player-week outcome frame:

* weekly expanding rolling-origin folds, and
* season-held-out folds (each test season is trained only on earlier seasons).

It reports player trailing mean (with position-history fallback) and position
historical mean baselines. The result includes prediction rows, overall,
position, and per-week MAE / empirical point-forecast CRPS (absolute error),
mean error, a point-forecast coverage diagnostic, Spearman rank, and top-five
recall. Fewer than three outer weeks is marked `inconclusive` and is not
promotable. Calendar keys, required columns, finite outcomes, positions, and
duplicate player-week keys fail closed. Input order and target values from an
outer test fold cannot influence its predictions.

Baseline policy is deliberately simple and explicit: each player trailing
mean expands over all strictly prior player observations, with minimum history
of one observation. A player without prior history falls back to the position
mean over strictly prior observations, then to the global mean over strictly
prior observations. No baseline run is promoted: at three or more outer weeks
the result is only `eligible_for_candidate_comparison`; fewer than three is
`inconclusive`.
