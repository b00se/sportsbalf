# R3.13-A direct-FP paired tournament

Status: implemented, unpromoted; independent review required before wave integration.

## Scope

`src/nfl/models/direct_fp_paired_tournament.py` compares the existing
leakage-safe direct fantasy-point evaluator with the declared
`player_trailing_mean` internal baseline. Weekly and season-held-out modes are
supported. Both evaluators receive the same deep copy of the caller's frame;
the tournament pairs their outputs on the canonical `(season, week,
player_id, position, outer_fold)` key. A missing or extra player-week key, a
duplicate, or a disagreement in actual outcome fails closed.

The result includes paired rows, per-outer-fold MAE and delta (`direct MAE -
internal MAE`), overall and position-by-fold slices, sample/fold counts, and a
seeded fold bootstrap interval. Fold deltas are equally weighted so a large
slate cannot dominate the outer-fold comparison.

## Conservative decision rule

The result always reports `status="unpromoted"`; this module never promotes a
candidate. `comparison_status` is `inconclusive` unless at least three outer
folds (or the configured `min_outer_folds`) are paired and the interval is
finite. A candidate is classified `conclusive_tie_or_better` only when the
upper confidence bound for `direct MAE - internal MAE` is at most zero. Thus a
negative delta favors direct FP, zero is a tie, and a positive upper bound is
`conclusive_worse`. The bootstrap interval is one-sided (`[0, 1-alpha]`) or
two-sided (`[alpha/2, 1-alpha/2]`) according to the explicit `alternative`
argument. Classification remains advisory and cannot unlock R4 by itself.
