# R3.14-B participation-provider availability cap

Status: Implemented; awaiting independent review

## Scope

`NFLReadPyProvider` now treats the current UTC calendar year as the maximum
source-available participation season. The boundary is evaluated at load time
through `_participation_max_season()`, rather than admitting one future season
from an import-time `year + 1` constant. Tests inject a fixed boundary and
verify that the current year remains accepted while the next year is rejected.

The 2016 lower bound, weeks 1-22, play identity validation, duplicate/raw-row
preservation, and typed unsupported legacy provider behavior are unchanged.
