# R3.15-A component candidate evaluator

Status: implemented, pending independent review.

The new `src.nfl.models.component_evaluation` module evaluates the approved
archived adapter, QB projector, RB projector, and half-PPR scoring transform
with weekly expanding-origin and season-held-out folds. Fold targets contain
only player identity, position, season, and week. Component and fantasy-point
MAE/CRPS are emitted overall and by position. Fewer than three outer folds is
`inconclusive` and never promotable. WR/TE are explicitly unsupported pending
route input and are not silently scored as candidate projections.
