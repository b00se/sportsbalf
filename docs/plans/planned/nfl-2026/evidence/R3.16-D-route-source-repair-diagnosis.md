## Attempts and evidence

The first repair deferred `posteam` validation until an eligible regular-season
pass-route candidate exists and added an exact weekly roster fallback for
missing participation alignment. Fixture RED/GREEN evidence was 7 passing
tests before the new cases and 9 passing afterward.

The second repair restricted roster validation to candidate season/week/team
keys, limited fallback to rows with missing alignment, and prevented a roster
entry from overriding a complete alignment that does not contain the PBP
receiver. Fixture RED/GREEN evidence was 9 passing tests before the new cases
and 11 passing afterward; Ruff and `git diff --check` passed.

The complete archived rerun still fails because 94 raw roster rows have blank
`gsis_id` values, including rows sharing a candidate calendar/team. Those rows
cannot prove any receiver identity and must not be globally accepted or used
as a substitute for a missing identity.

## Required future remedy

Before strict roster field validation, select roster rows only by exact
candidate `(season, week, posteam, receiver_player_id)` identity keys. Then
validate nonempty ID, team, position, calendar, and uniqueness on that exact
subset. This safely discards non-matchable blank-ID roster rows while retaining
fail-closed behavior for any candidate identity that has malformed or
ambiguous roster evidence. It must be implemented with regression coverage and
re-run against these manifests.

No third materially similar repair was attempted in this task. No receiver
route coverage, WR/TE projection, ranking, or promotion claim follows from
these attempts. R4 remains blocked.
