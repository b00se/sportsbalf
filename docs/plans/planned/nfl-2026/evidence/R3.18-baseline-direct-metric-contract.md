# R3.18: Naive and direct-FP metric contract

Status: Complete for comparator reporting. This does not promote a candidate
or permit R4.

## Contract alignment

The internal naïve and direct fantasy-point evaluators now use the same long
metric schema as the component evaluator. Each row identifies its
`aggregation` (`fold` or `aggregate`), `outer_folds`, `valid_folds`, status,
definition, and reason. Aggregate values are `NaN` and `inconclusive` unless
at least three distinct outer folds are valid.

Spearman and top-k recall are computed independently within each outer fold,
then averaged across valid folds. Top-k uses `k=min(3, fold size)` and resolves
ties by ascending `player_id`. Point-forecast CRPS is explicitly MAE.
Calibration is explicitly unavailable: neither comparator emits predictive
intervals or probabilities, so the former absolute-error `coverage` field was
removed rather than mislabeled as calibration.

In weekly mode, an outer fold is a `season-week` slate. In season-held-out
mode, an outer fold is one held-out season and ranking is across that season's
weekly player rows; it is not a per-week ranking result. Two source seasons
therefore provide only one evaluable season fold and remain inconclusive.
