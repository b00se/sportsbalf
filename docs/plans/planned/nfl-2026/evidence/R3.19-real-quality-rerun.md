# R3.19: Fresh real-quality rerun

Status: Evidence captured; candidates unpromoted; R4 blocked.

## Scope and decision

This packet records a fresh, cache-only rerun of the archived NFLverse weekly
outcomes and the three deterministic evaluation paths available in R3:

* `player_trailing_mean`, the declared internal primary baseline;
* direct fantasy-point projection; and
* the archived component projector, which currently has only QB/RB inputs.

The run uses half-PPR scoring, seasons 2021--2025, and strict rolling-origin
evaluation. Weekly folds are season-week slates; season folds hold out one
complete season. Every aggregate reported below has at least three outer folds.
The component results are not an all-position result: their 11,339 weekly and
9,192 season predictions cover QB/RB only.

This is an **all-slate preliminary** rerun, not a regular-season-only result.
The archived weekly source contains 90,585 regular-season rows and 4,263
postseason rows (`season_type` = `REG`/`POST`). The 109 weekly folds include
the available calendar slates through week 22. An apples-to-apples regular
season-only rerun is still required before using these numbers as the final
WR/TE comparison.

The explicit decision is **R4 blocked**. No candidate is promoted. The direct
FP result is useful as a comparator, but its seasonal MAE is worse than the
internal primary baseline. The component candidate is not comparable across
all positions and is also worse on the QB/RB fantasy-point subset. Historical
Underdog and consensus projections were not available, and deterministic point
forecasts do not provide probabilistic calibration.

## Calibration, external baselines, and limitations

All three paths emit deterministic point forecasts. Their point-forecast CRPS
is therefore exactly absolute error/MAE. Calibration is **unavailable**: no
predictive intervals or probabilities were emitted, so no probability or
interval calibration claim is made. The evaluator marks those calibration rows
`inconclusive` rather than treating an error-threshold coverage diagnostic as
calibration.

Underdog and consensus historical projection archives remain unavailable.
Current-slate values cannot substitute for retrospective as-of folds and are
not included. The all-slate result must not be conflated with a regular-season
benchmark; postseason inclusion is a known scope limitation. The route
materializer currently provides no accepted WR/TE
component candidate; the component table is strictly QB/RB. Direct and
component outputs are in-memory and therefore have less artifact-level audit
depth than the persisted baseline runner outputs.

The rerun has meaningful historical coverage and eligible fold counts, but it
does not clear candidate promotion: no candidate ties/beats the best declared
baseline on the required complete comparison, the direct seasonal result is
worse, and calibration plus external-baseline evidence is missing. **R4
blocked.**
