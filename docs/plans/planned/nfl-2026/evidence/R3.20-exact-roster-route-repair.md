# R3.20: Exact-identity receiver-route repair

Status: Complete for conservative route materialization. It does not promote a WR/TE projection or permit R4.

## Repair contract

R3.16-D established that weekly rosters can contain incomplete rows unrelated to eligible receiver-route candidates. The repair filters roster records by the full candidate key `(season, week, posteam, receiver_player_id)` before validating roster fields. A blank or malformed roster identity that cannot match a candidate is discarded; a matching malformed or duplicate record still fails closed.

Roster fallback is used only when the participation player/position alignment cells are missing. If both alignment lists are present and omit the PBP receiver, the receiver stays unlinked even if a roster has an apparent match. `posteam` is validated only after a row is a regular-season pass-route candidate. There is no outcome-derived position or cross-week imputation.

The immutable 2021--2025 NFLverse source rerun contained 238,135 participation rows, 247,284 PBP rows, and 231,942 weekly-roster rows. Materialization returned 16,012 WR/TE player-weeks for 653 players and 69,950 identified route rows: 51,103 WR and 18,847 TE. Diagnostics were 87,302 regular-season route candidates, 86,069 position-linked rows, 1,233 unlinked rows, 16,119 excluded non-WR/TE rows, and zero unmatched participation route keys.

The independent derivation matched those counts. Of the 1,233 unlinked rows, 1,214 lack a PBP receiver identity and 19 have a complete participation alignment that omits that receiver. Both remain excluded.

Fresh manifest-backed participation/PBP/roster caches are being retained outside the repository for the following receiver evaluator run. This packet does not treat the materialized lower bound as complete routes run, and R4 remains blocked pending leakage-safe WR/TE projections, comparison evidence, probabilistic calibration, and unavailable market baselines.
