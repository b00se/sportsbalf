# R3.21: Regular-season quality rerun

Status: Evidence captured; candidates unpromoted; R4 blocked.

## Scope and decision

This packet records the fresh cache-only rerun of the archived NFLverse
regular-season weekly outcomes and the deterministic R3 evaluation paths:

* `player_trailing_mean`, the declared internal primary baseline;
* direct fantasy-point projection; and
* the archived component projector, which currently has QB/RB coverage only.

The run uses half-PPR scoring, seasons 2021--2025, and strict rolling-origin
evaluation. Weekly folds are season-week slates and season folds hold out one
complete season. The regular-season source produces 89 weekly outer folds and
four held-out season outer folds. Every aggregate result reported here has at
least three outer folds.

Baseline and direct-FP evaluation include the full archived QB/RB/WR/TE
outcome population. The component path is not an all-position result: it has
10,837 weekly and 8,790 season predictions, and those predictions are QB/RB
only. No identified-route WR/TE component candidate was evaluated in this
rerun. Therefore this packet is the apples-to-apples regular-season comparator
rerun, not evidence that the WR/TE component path is complete.

The explicit decision is **R4 blocked**. No candidate is promoted. Direct FP
improves weekly MAE by 0.131803 versus the internal trailing-mean baseline but
is worse on season-held-out MAE by 0.127635. The component result is not
comparable across all positions and is worse than the corresponding baseline
on the QB/RB fantasy-point subset. Historical Underdog and consensus
projection archives remain unavailable, and deterministic point forecasts do
not provide probabilistic calibration.

## Calibration, external baselines, and limitations

All paths emit deterministic point forecasts. Their point-forecast CRPS is
therefore exactly MAE. Calibration is **unavailable**: no predictive
intervals or probabilities were emitted, so no probability or interval
calibration claim is made. The evaluator marks calibration rows
`inconclusive` rather than treating an error-threshold coverage diagnostic as
calibration.

Historical Underdog and consensus projection archives were not available.
Current-slate values cannot substitute for retrospective as-of folds and are
not included. The identified-route materializer has not yet yielded an
accepted WR/TE component candidate for this rerun, so no WR/TE component
promotion claim is made. The component candidate remains strictly QB/RB.

The regular-season rerun has meaningful historical coverage and eligible fold
counts, but it does not clear candidate promotion: no candidate has a complete
comparison against the required best baseline with the missing external
baselines and WR/TE candidate, and direct FP is worse on the seasonal horizon.
**R4 blocked.**
