# R3.6 Availability Mixtures

Status: complete

## Scope

- `src/nfl/models/availability_mixtures.py`
- `tests/test_nfl_availability_mixtures.py`
- this evidence record

The contract represents conditional active and inactive component outcomes as
a Bernoulli mixture. Confirmed inactive/out status is a zero point mass and
cannot remain selection-eligible. Uncertain availability is probability
weighted independently from conditional production volatility. Exact mixture
mean and variance, bounded weighted empirical quantiles, and optional seeded
samples are returned; the seed is surfaced in the result.

Calibration evaluation is transparent and non-promoting by design. If
historical probabilities/outcomes are supplied, it reports Brier score and
expected calibration error, strictly excludes rows at or after an explicit
cutoff, and never claims promotion. No direct fantasy scoring or automation
is included.

## Exit-criteria mapping

1. `mixture_mean` equals `P(active) * active_mean + (1-P(active)) * inactive_mean`.
2. `mixture_variance` uses the law of total variance and separates availability
   risk from conditional performance volatility.
3. Confirmed inactive status forces probability and all quantiles to zero.
4. Invalid, nonfinite, negative, boolean, and out-of-range inputs fail closed.
5. Sampling is reproducible with an explicit surfaced seed.
6. Calibration is cutoff-safe, target-fold independent, reported, and remains
   `promoted=False` absent valid comparative rolling-origin evidence.

## Independent review repair 1

The Luna-high review identified four edge cases. Weighted quantiles now remove
zero-weight support and clamp cumulative rounding at both endpoints; calibration
strictly scores only known binary outcomes before the cutoff; overflow in the
law-of-total-variance calculation fails closed; and direct/configured seeds
reject fractional, boolean, nonfinite, or otherwise non-integral values rather
than truncating. Boolean component and outcome observations are rejected.
