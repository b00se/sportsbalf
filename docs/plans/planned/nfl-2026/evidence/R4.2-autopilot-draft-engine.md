# R4.2 Exact Autopilot Draft Engine

Status: Implemented (offline simulator; no draft, upload, account, or entry automation)

## Scope and contract

`src/nfl/models/autopilot_draft.py` simulates exactly six rounds for exactly
four or six entrants using the typed `NflContestConfig` contract. Picks follow
explicit serpentine order and choose the lowest rank (then highest projection,
then a seeded deterministic tie order) among position-cap and roster-feasible
players. Player pools may contain `DraftPlayer` values or mappings with
`player_id`/`id`, `position`, `rank`, and optional `projection`.

Every player ID is validated as unique and every completed roster has
1 QB/1 RB/2 WR/1 TE/1 FLEX. FLEX is assigned deterministically to the one
additional RB/WR/TE. The engine raises `DraftValidationError` for unsupported
room sizes, non-six-round contracts, malformed players, duplicate IDs, pools
too small to draft, or a pick with no legal candidate. Results expose only
safe audit metadata: seed, entrant/round counts, complete entrant order, and
`automated_actions: false`.
