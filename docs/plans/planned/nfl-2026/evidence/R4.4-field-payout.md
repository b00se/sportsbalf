# R4.4 Field and Exact Payout Simulator

Status: Implemented (offline only; no draft, account, entry, upload, or live-service operations)

`src/nfl/models/field_simulation.py` composes the typed R4.1, R4.2, and R4.3
outputs. It generates one exact Autopilot draft room per archived-ADP
scenario, scores every legal roster on every fixed-seed joint direct-FP path,
and returns auditable entry rows containing room/scenario IDs, score, rank,
tie group, tie size, and `Decimal` cent payouts. The result audit retains the
archived-ADP and non-promoted direct-FP provenance and explicitly declares
`automated_actions: false`, `live_service: false`, and
`account_or_entry_operations: false`.

The target field defaults to the typed contest field size. Four-entrant rooms
produce exactly 500 entries for the standard 500-entry contract. When a target
is not divisible by the room size, the largest compatible field not exceeding
the target is used and reported via `target_field_size`, `actual_field_size`,
and `approximation_permitted`; an approximation that would exclude a configured
payout rank fails closed.

Payouts are expanded from the typed `NflContestConfig.payouts.ladder`. A tie
occupies its consecutive rank slots and the aggregate occupied payout is split
in cents, with remainder cents assigned by deterministic entry ID order. Each
simulation therefore conserves the configured prize pool exactly.
