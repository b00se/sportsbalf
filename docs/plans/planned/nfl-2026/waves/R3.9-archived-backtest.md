# Wave R3.9 — Archived End-to-End Backtest Gate

Status: Planned; R4 blocked pending completion

## Objective

Run a reproducible, archived NFL weekly backtest from ingestion through
as-of feature construction, component projections, fantasy scoring, and
rankings. The result must establish forecast and ranking quality on untouched
historical slates before R4 begins. This packet is a validation gate, not a
model or provider implementation packet.

## Required historical inputs

The run must use immutable, locally addressable snapshots with a manifest for
each input (source, retrieval timestamp, schema/version, row count, byte
digest, and license/terms evidence):

1. NFL schedules, player outcomes, identities, availability, and the raw or
   normalized feature inputs needed to rebuild internal features.
2. Historical Underdog projections for the same players/slates, including the
   as-of timestamp and canonical identity mapping.
3. Historical independent-consensus projections for the same players/slates,
   including the as-of timestamp, source lineage, terms evidence, and mapping.

The currently checked-in projection CSVs are two-row Week-0/Week-1 fixtures
for one player, not a multi-season archive. They can test contracts only and
cannot satisfy this gate. A missing source is recorded as unavailable; no
synthetic replacement, current projection, or post hoc reconstruction may be
called historical evidence.

## Evaluation protocol

- Choose a documented set of seasons and weekly slate cutoffs. For every test
  slate, rebuild ingestion normalization, identity resolution, availability,
  features, components, fantasy points, and rankings using only snapshots
  timestamped at or before that slate's cutoff.
- Use strict rolling-origin folds: training observations must be strictly
  earlier than the test slate, and model/blend/feature selection must occur
  inside training/inner-validation data only. The test slate's outcomes and
  predictions are write-once evaluation inputs, never selection inputs.
- Report results only when there are at least three distinct outer weeks. A
  smaller result is explicitly `inconclusive` and blocks R4, regardless of
  player-row count.
- Preserve fold-level outputs and source-manifest references so another
  operator can rerun the exact evaluation without network access.

## Metrics and declared baselines

Report overall and by QB/RB/WR/TE (and any supported position grouping):

- component MAE and fantasy-point MAE;
- deterministic empirical CRPS for point/distribution forecasts;
- Spearman rank correlation and top-k recall for rankings; and
- calibration (with sample counts and uncertainty) for every reported
  probability/confidence output.

The internal naïve baseline is mandatory and must be scored as its own
candidate in every outer fold: for each player and target slate, predict the
arithmetic mean of that player's eligible observed fantasy-point outcomes
strictly before the fold (or a pre-declared trailing window over those prior
observations). The window, minimum-history rule, and any explicitly labeled
hierarchical fallback are fixed before evaluation; no target-fold outcome or
prediction may affect them. This historical/trailing-mean baseline is not a
component projection and is not the direct-fantasy-point candidate.

Score the internal naïve historical/trailing mean, each internal component
candidate, the direct-fantasy-point candidate, Underdog, independent
consensus, and any ensemble separately in every outer-fold comparison.
Preserve candidate/source identity and coverage for every comparison; do not
silently score missing baselines.

## Promotion rule and R4 gate

A candidate is eligible to promote only if it ties or beats the best declared
baseline on outer-fold fantasy-point MAE, has no inconclusive confidence
interval or insufficient-fold result, and passes the leakage/as-of audit.
Otherwise it remains unpromoted. The R4 gate is `R4 allowed` only after all
required inputs, manifests, fold-level metrics, aggregate report, and the
decision record exist; it is `R4 blocked` for missing archives, unavailable
baselines, fewer than three outer weeks, leakage, failed calibration, or
non-conclusive comparisons.

## Reproducibility deliverables

The completed packet must include the exact command and environment, source
manifest paths and digests, fold definitions, fold-level result files,
aggregate metrics/report, coverage and missingness, uncertainty method, and
an explicit final `R4 allowed` or `R4 blocked` decision. It must record which
external source terms permit local retention and reuse. Raw data acquisition
is outside this docs-only packet and requires approved immutable snapshots and
terms before implementation.

## Boundary

This packet does not begin R4, change production code, alter protected data
directories, or claim predictive edge. Once the archives and terms are
available, implementation may be assigned as a narrowly scoped R3.9 task;
until then the correct outcome is a documented block.
